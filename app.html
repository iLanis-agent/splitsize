SplitSize

How many visitors an A/B test needs before the result means anything, and whether yours already does.

2. Check a finished test

Method: standard two-sided two-proportion z-test, normal approximation, equal split. Visitors per variant = (z(alpha/2) + z(power))^2 x (p1(1-p1) + p2(1-p2)) / (p2-p1)^2. Check: 10% to 12%, 5% significance, 80% power gives 3,839 per variant here; one published worked example (Metricgate) states 3,841, a 0.05% difference from rounding the z values. Other calculators (for example Evan Miller's) use different methods and can differ by a few percent or more. More than 2 variants only multiplies the traffic: no multiple-comparison correction. Decide the sample size in advance and read the result once at the end: peeking early and stopping on a good-looking p-value inflates false positives. A low p-value says a difference is unlikely to be chance, not that it matters to your business. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
